My time there was focused on implementing and extending the *Mapper* algorithm, a topological method that reframes common data analysis tasks as problems of analyzing [level sets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Level_set) on topological spaces (introduction available [here](https://www.quantmetry.com/blog/topological-data-analysis-with-mapper/)).

Beyond learning the theory and developing the software, I also applied *Mapper* to various geospatial and image analysis tasks, such as video segmentation, object recognition, and clustering.
